## Question

What exactly is a gathered fact in Argument Maker, where does the collection live, and which create, edit, order, duplicate, and delete behaviors are required before argument construction begins?

## Resolution requirements

- Name the user-facing concept in plain language and define its relationship to the existing Supporting Data or Facts rows.
- Decide whether gathered facts belong to one Argument Board, a separately importable collection, or another local-only boundary.
- Decide the minimum fields: fact text, Evidence Link, Data Type, and any genuinely required source note or label.
- Decide whether incomplete drafts are allowed and which fields make an item complete enough to select.
- Decide ordering, deletion, duplication, and empty-state behavior without adding speculative organization features.
- State whether gathered facts may feed only Supporting Arguments or also Situation and Complication.
- Preserve the rule that a stored link is traceability, not proof of truth.
