# 07: Provide an accessible Reasoning review workspace

**What to build:** An author can open a focused Reasoning review at any time to consider structural gaps, Audience, Intended outcome, and the reasoning behind the Answer. The review supports reflection without certifying the argument or blocking other work.

**Blocked by:** 04: Capture and preserve Audience and Intended outcome.

**Status:** complete (local; no external tracker configured)

- [x] Provide a discoverable, independently accessible way to open and leave Reasoning review without first attempting an export.
- [x] Show current structural gaps together with the saved Audience and Intended outcome, handling absent optional purpose fields clearly.
- [x] Present existing reflection prompts about how reasoning supports the Answer, assumptions, an important objection, and what could weaken the claim or change the conclusion.
- [x] Authors can revise their Reasoning notes through the review; changes stay consistent with the existing argument editors and participate in normal history and persistence.
- [x] Reflection fields remain optional, and entering or leaving review does not block editing, navigation, or export.
- [x] Clearly distinguish structural completeness from the author's judgment; do not award a verified badge, quality score, or persistent certification.

- [x] Verify opening review, editing notes, leaving, and returning through the browser; use focused public history/persistence checks only for additional meaningful edge cases.
